============================================================
MANDATORY RULES
============================================================
- Do NOT invent impact. Describe only impacts supported by evidence.
- Do NOT use generic "The impact is unknown" text.
- Do NOT state that a product, patient, or process was affected unless VERIFIED in the ledger.
- Never state a duration, date, or count not present in the finding text or evidence ledger.
- Do NOT mention product safety, patient safety, recall, quarantine, or regulatory reporting
  unless the finding itself gives a basis for it.
- IMPACT_REQUIRES_ASSESSMENT does NOT mean you write nothing. You must still identify the
  affected object, affected people, and potential process effect from this finding's own facts.

============================================================
RULE 18 — NEVER ASSUME WHAT CHANGED
============================================================
If the finding says only "the procedure was revised" or "a new version was issued",
do NOT state what the revision changed. You do not know.
Instead write: "Determine what requirements changed between the obsolete and current versions."
Only describe specific changes if the finding explicitly states them.

============================================================
RULE 19 — DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY INTRODUCE TRAINING
============================================================
Training is relevant to impact ONLY if:
- explicitly stated in the finding, OR
- required by the applicable procedure (and that requirement is stated), OR
- established as a factor during the investigation.
Do NOT mention training impact for a document-control or procedural finding.

============================================================
STRUCTURED IMPACT REASONING — REASON THROUGH EACH FIELD
============================================================

1. AFFECTED_OBJECT: What specific process, record, output, or asset is directly implicated?
   Name the actual thing using this finding's own entities. Not a generic category.

2. AFFECTED_PEOPLE: Who specifically was affected? Use only entities named in the finding
   (e.g. "eight employees across two departments"). If count is unknown, say so.

3. AFFECTED_PERIOD: What time period is stated in the finding? If no period is given, write
   "requires confirmation — no period stated in finding."

4. PROCESS_AT_RISK: What downstream process or decision depends on the affected object?
   Only state this if the finding gives a basis for it. If not, write "not determinable from
   available evidence."

5. RELEVANT_CHANGE: What specifically changed that is relevant to potential impact?
   If the finding does NOT state what changed, write: "Determine what requirements changed
   between the superseded and current versions — do not assume."

6. POTENTIAL_EFFECT: What could plausibly be wrong or compromised?
   Base this ONLY on what the finding states. Label it "Potential effect (unconfirmed)".
   Never assert certainty.

7. EVIDENCE_NEEDED: What specific record or review would bound the scope of impact?
   Be specific — name the record type and what it would confirm.

NARRATIVE: One paragraph using only this finding's own entities and (if stated) timeframe.
State what is potentially affected and why the scope cannot yet be determined.
