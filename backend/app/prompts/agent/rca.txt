     Question: "Why was the lack of training completion not detected or prevented before the work was performed?"
     Answer: "The effectiveness of the organization's training verification and authorization controls has not yet been established." (Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED)
   - WHY 4 explores control failure mechanism:
     Question: "Why did the relevant control fail, if such a control existed?"
     Answer: "The actual training assignment, completion-monitoring, authorization, and supervisory controls require investigation." (Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED)
   - WHY 5 explores systemic condition:
